## 8. HERO WEBSITE CONCEPT — MAJOR CREATIVE IDEA

A jewelry chain drops into the ocean.

The camera follows the chain downward underwater.

We descend through the water. We see sunlight/rays above.

The chain reaches the ocean floor.

When it lands, the experience transitions into the actual product collection.

The products appear as though they are naturally lying on the ocean floor.

The visitor then scrolls through the collection/world.

Desired emotional feeling:
- discovery
- underwater treasure
- exploration
- premium editorial campaign
- ocean
- mystery
- jewelry as objects discovered rather than products dumped into a grid

Do NOT make this feel like a gimmicky video game.

It must remain fast, elegant, premium, usable, conversion-focused, and mobile-conscious.

If full 3D is too heavy/fragile, propose alternatives:
- pre-rendered cinematic video
- image sequence
- GSAP/scroll-driven animation
- WebGL/Three.js only where justified
- hybrid video + DOM UI
- progressive enhancement
- static/mobile fallback

Do not build unnecessarily heavy 3D just because it is possible.
